Questions

Frequently asked.

Everything you might want to know before jumping on a call.

How long does it actually take to build an agent?
For pre-built agents (Support, Sales, Content) — 7 days from kick-off. For fully custom agents with unique workflows — 10 days. If we miss the agreed date, you get a 100% refund of everything you have paid. That's the promise.
Who owns the agent and code after it's built?
You do. 100%. Everything is deployed on your infrastructure — your VPS, your API keys, your accounts. We hand over full documentation and access. No lock-in, no monthly rent for the code.
What tools and integrations do you support?
Pretty much anything with an API. Common ones: WhatsApp Business, Slack, Gmail, Google Workspace, HubSpot, Zoho, Notion, Airtable, Shopify, Razorpay, Stripe, Meta Ads, LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, custom databases. If it has an API, we can hook it in.
What are the ongoing costs after the one-time fee?
Just the actual API and hosting costs — usually ₹500 to ₹3,000 per month depending on volume (LLM API + VPS + third-party services). We use a BYOK model, so you pay providers directly. No hidden markups from us.
Can you build in Hindi or regional languages?
Absolutely. Most of our WhatsApp bots handle Hindi, Hinglish, English, and can be extended to Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, etc. Voice agents work in native languages too via ElevenLabs and similar.
What if I'm not technical? Can I still use this?
Yes — most of our clients aren't technical. We handle the entire setup and give you a simple dashboard or Slack/WhatsApp interface to interact with your agents. Zero code required from you.
What happens on the free discovery call?
30 minutes. We understand your business, identify your top 2–3 automation opportunities, and give you a rough estimate of hours and cost saved. Even if you don't work with us, you walk away with a clear roadmap.
